About us

Redlands Local, Family-Run Lawn Care for 10+ Years

Golden Cane Lawns is an independent, family-run lawn and garden maintenance business based in Wellington Point, run by owner Levi Engstrom.

Our story

A local business, not a franchise

We're Redlands locals. We live here, our kids grow up here, and the lawns we look after belong to our neighbours.

Levi has been mowing, trimming and maintaining gardens for over a decade. Golden Cane Lawns grew from that hands-on experience into an independent, family-run business that looks after homes, rentals and properties across Wellington Point and the Redlands bayside.

Being independent means you deal directly with the people doing the work. No call centres, no franchise fees built into your price, and no rotating crew who've never seen your yard before.

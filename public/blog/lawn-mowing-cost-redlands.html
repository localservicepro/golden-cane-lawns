How Much Does Lawn Mowing Cost in the Redlands? (2026 Guide)

Block size, grass condition, access and how often you mow all change the price. Here's how lawn mowing is priced in the Redlands, and how to keep it down.

The honest answer to "how much does lawn mowing cost?" is: it depends on your lawn. Two houses on the same street in Wellington Point can have very different prices because of block size, slope, access and how long since the grass was last cut. This guide explains exactly what goes into a lawn mowing quote in the Redlands, so you know what you're paying for and how to keep the cost down.

What affects the price of lawn mowing?

1. The size of the mowable area

The biggest factor is how much grass there is to cut, not the size of the block. A 600 m² block with a pool, big garden beds and a driveway might have less lawn than a 450 m² block that's all grass. Most local operators quote on the time the job takes, and time is driven mostly by mowable area.

2. How long since it was last mowed

Long, thick grass takes far longer to cut, fills the catcher several times over and produces much more green waste. That's why a one-off cut of an overgrown lawn costs noticeably more than a regular mow of the same yard. In the Redlands' wet season, a lawn can go from tidy to overgrown in three or four weeks.

3. Access, slope and obstacles

Narrow side gates, steps, steep embankments, lots of garden beds and trees to work around, or a backyard that can only be reached through the house all add time. Flat, open lawns with easy access are the quickest and cheapest to mow.

4. What's included

Check what a quote actually covers. At Golden Cane Lawns every mow includes edging, whipper snipping, blowing down paths and driveways, and green waste removal. Some cheaper quotes leave clippings on site or charge extra for edges, which makes them harder to compare.

5. How often you book

Regular customers almost always pay less per visit. A lawn mowed every two or three weeks is quicker to cut and leaves less waste than one cut every six to eight weeks, and regular rounds let us plan efficient routes through Wellington Point, Ormiston, Cleveland and the rest of the Redlands.

One-off mows vs regular lawn mowing

A one-off mow makes sense for an end-of-lease clean, a pre-sale tidy-up, or catching up after holidays. For everyday upkeep a regular round is better value and better for the lawn: cutting little and often keeps grass thicker and healthier, which in turn crowds out weeds. Most Redlands customers go fortnightly from September to April and every three to four weeks through winter.

How to keep your lawn mowing cost down

  • Book a regular round rather than waiting until the lawn is long.
  • Clear the yard of toys, hoses, dog bowls and furniture before the mower arrives.
  • Make access easy: unlock gates and let us know about pets.
  • Bundle jobs: having hedge trimming or weed spraying done on the same visit avoids paying for separate call-outs.

Why we don't publish a one-size price list

Because every lawn is different, a flat price list either overcharges people with small, easy lawns or undercharges for big, difficult ones and gets "adjusted" on the day. Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to mow fortnightly or monthly?

Per visit, fortnightly is usually cheaper because the grass is shorter and quicker to cut with less green waste. Over a year the totals often end up similar, but the lawn is in much better condition on a regular schedule.

Does the price include taking the clippings away?

With Golden Cane Lawns, yes. Edging, whipper snipping, blow-down and green waste removal are included in every mow. Always check what's included when comparing quotes.

Why does an overgrown lawn cost more?

Long grass takes much longer to cut, often needs a second pass, and produces several times more green waste to remove.

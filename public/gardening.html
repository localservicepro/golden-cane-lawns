Garden maintenance FAQs

What does garden maintenance include?

Weeding, pruning and shaping shrubs, cutting back overgrowth, mulching, tidying beds and removing all the green waste. We tailor each visit to what your garden needs.

Do you offer regular garden maintenance in Cleveland?

Yes. We offer weekly, fortnightly, monthly or seasonal visits in Cleveland and across the Redlands, and garden work can be combined with your lawn mowing.

Can you do a one-off garden tidy-up before selling or moving out?

Yes. We do one-off pre-sale and end-of-lease garden tidy-ups, usually combined with mowing, edging and hedge trimming so the whole property is ready.

Do you remove the garden waste?

Yes. Green waste removal is included and we leave paths and lawns blown clean.

Which suburbs do you cover for garden maintenance?

We cover Wellington Point, Ormiston, Cleveland, Thornlands, Alexandra Hills, Capalaba and Birkdale.

A well-kept hedge does a lot of work: privacy from the street and the neighbours, shelter from wind, and a clean frame for the rest of your garden. Left alone through a Redlands summer it can get leggy, patchy and out of control quickly. We trim to keep hedges dense, even and the size you want them.

How often should hedges be trimmed?

In the Redlands' warm, wet growing season most hedges need a trim every four to eight weeks to stay tight. Fast growers such as murraya and lilly pilly sit at the shorter end; slower species can go longer. Through the cooler months every eight to twelve weeks is usually enough. Regular light trims keep a hedge thicker and healthier than an occasional hard cut. Read our season-by-season hedge trimming guide for South-East Queensland for more detail.

One-off hedge reshapes

Moving in, selling up, or a hedge that's been left too long? We do one-off reshapes to bring overgrown hedges back to a clean shape and a manageable height, then you can keep them there with a regular trim. Combine it with lawn mowing and weed spraying for a full property tidy-up in one visit.

Hedge trimming FAQs

How much does hedge trimming cost in the Redlands?

It depends on the length, height and condition of the hedge and how easy it is to get to. We give free, no-obligation quotes, and regular trims cost less per visit than a big reshape of an overgrown hedge.

Do you take the hedge clippings away?

Yes. Clean-up and green waste removal are included. We rake and blow down paths, lawns and garden beds before we leave.

When is the best time to trim hedges in South-East Queensland?

Light trims through the growing season (spring to autumn) keep hedges dense. Heavier reshapes are best done in late winter or early spring, just before strong new growth starts.

Can you trim tall hedges and privacy screens?

Yes. We trim tall privacy screens and boundary hedges. Very tall or hard-to-access hedges are quoted after a quick look so we can plan safe access.

Yes, along with Ormiston, Alexandra Hills, Capalaba, Birkdale.

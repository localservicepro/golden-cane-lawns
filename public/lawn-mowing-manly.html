Slopes, views and character homes in Manly

Manly is one of the prettiest corners of the bayside, and many of its streets climb up from the waterfront. Sloping blocks, retaining walls and embankments are common, and they're where a lot of the work is: steep banks are often safer and neater with careful whipper snipping than with a mower, and the edges along steps and walls need a steady hand.

Like Wynnum, Manly is in the Brisbane City Council area, and we include it in our regular rounds alongside the Redlands suburbs just across Tingalpa Creek.

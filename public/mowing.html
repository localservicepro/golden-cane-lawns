Regular lawn mowing rounds

Most Redlands lawns grow hard from September to April. A regular round keeps the grass at a consistent height, which means a healthier, denser lawn, fewer weeds and a lower cost per visit than letting it get away and paying for a big catch-up cut. Through summer most customers choose a fortnightly mow; through winter, every three to four weeks is usually plenty.

One-off mows for rentals, sales and catch-ups

We also do one-off lawn mowing for tenants, landlords and property managers in Redland Bay, Thornlands, Cleveland and the rest of the Redlands: end-of-lease cleans, routine inspections, pre-sale tidy-ups and overgrown lawns that need bringing back under control. Add hedge trimming, weed spraying or a garden tidy-up to the same visit to get the whole property presentation-ready.

Whipper snipping

Whipper snipping (line trimming) is what makes a mowed lawn look finished. We trim along fence lines, around trees, posts, letterboxes and garden edges, along verges and down the side of the house where a mower can't get in. It's included with every mow, and we also do whipper snipping on its own for verges, nature strips, embankments and overgrown side paths across the Redlands.

Lawn mowing FAQs

How often should my lawn be mowed in the Redlands?

From September to April most Redlands lawns need mowing every one to two weeks; through the cooler months every three to four weeks is usually enough. We'll recommend a schedule based on your grass type and how quickly it grows.

Do you take the clippings away?

Yes. Green waste removal is included, and we blow down your paths, driveway and patio before we leave.

Is whipper snipping and edging included?

Yes. Every mow includes edging along hard surfaces and whipper snipping around fences, trees and garden edges.

Do you mow lawns in Capalaba and Victoria Point?

Yes. We mow lawns right across the Redlands including Capalaba and Victoria Point, as well as Wellington Point, Ormiston, Cleveland, Thornlands, Redland Bay, Birkdale, Thorneside, Wynnum, Manly.

Can you do a one-off mow for an overgrown lawn or rental?

Yes. We do one-off mows for overgrown lawns, end-of-lease cleans, inspections and pre-sale tidy-ups. Overgrown lawns take longer, so they're quoted separately from a regular mow.
